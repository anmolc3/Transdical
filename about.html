About Transdical Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Advancing Science. Ensuring Quality

Transdical Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a leading analytical testing and scientific solutions provider committed to delivering accurate, reliable, and timely results. We empower industries and researchers with advanced testing services, technical expertise, and end-to-end laboratory solutions.

Who We Are

Your Trusted Partner in Analytical Excellence

Transdical Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (TTPL) provides world-class analytical, scientific and laboratory solutions. Our solutions support a wide range of industries including Pharmaceutical, Biologics, Food, Formulation, Textile, and Advanced Research.

With a team of experienced scientists, state-of-the-art instruments, and a commitment to quality, we deliver results you can trust.
