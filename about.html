Who We Are

Your Strategic Partner in Scientific Innovation

Transdical Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (TTPL) is a scientific solutions, technology, and analytical partner dedicated to helping organizations overcome technical, analytical, and operational challenges. We bridge the gap between scientific challenges and practical solutions by combining deep technical domain expertise, strategic technology access, high-quality products, and an extensive network of specialized partners.

Rather than operating purely as a conventional laboratory or supplier, TTPL works collaboratively with clients to understand requirements, evaluate options, and deliver integrated scientific solutions.
