About Transdical Pvt. Ltd

Advancing Science.Ensuring Quality

Transdical Pvt. Ltd. is a leading analytical testing and scientific solutions provider committed to delivering accurate, reliable, and timely results. We empower industries and researchers with advanced testing services, technical expertise, and end-to-end laboratory solutions.

Your Trusted Partner in Analytical Excellence

Our services support a wide range of industries including Food, Pharmaceutical, Drug, Cosmetic, Leather, Textile, Environmental, Chemical, and more.
